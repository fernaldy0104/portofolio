PROJECT 03

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PROJECT – BINUS UNIVERSITY

OVERVIEW:

An AI-driven early warning system using Random Forest Classifier to predict flood probabilities across Central Jakarta based on 16 multivariate parameters (environmental, infrastructure, urban, and governance factors).

KEY FEATURES:

  • • Multivariate Data Modeling: Evaluated 16 critical risk parameters across 4 domains.
  • • Random Forest Classification: Machine learning model outputting exact risk levels (Low to Critical).
  • • Geospatial Dashboard: Interactive mapping with color-coded risk zone visualizer.
  • • Trend Analysis: Dedicated analytics page tracking weekly and monthly flood risks.

SUPERSTORE SALES TIME SERIES & TREND ANALYTICS

PROJECT 04

DATA VISUALIZATION PROJECT – BINUS UNIVERSITY

OVERVIEW:

Analyzed 9,800 US retail sales transactions over a 4-year period (2015–2018) using Tableau to conduct time series analysis, uncovering long-term revenue growth, Q4 seasonality, and sales volatility.

KEY FEATURES:

  • • Time Series & Trend Analysis: Constructed linear trendlines proving long-term revenue expansion.
  • • Seasonality Recognition: Identified annual sales spikes during November holiday seasons.
  • • Moving Average Smoothing: Filtered short-term metric noise for cleaner executive reports.
  • • Interactive Tableau Visuals: Multi-dimensional filtering across regions and categories.
TECH STACK
Tableau Data Visualization Time Series Analysis Excel / SQL
